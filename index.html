Built for students & coordinators

👥

Smart Group Formation

Students can form, browse, and join course-based groups while the system guards against duplicate memberships and invalid data.

📋

Task & Deadline Tracking

Assignments and coursework timelines are surfaced with completion status, missed work, and nearest deadline countdowns.

📊

Live Oversight

Coordinators monitor rosters, unassigned students, group balance and course unit health in one place.

🔄

Dynamic University Rules

Registration number validation is flexible per university, faculty, intake, program and course structure.

🛠️

Intervention Tools

Force-add members, lock group creation, reassign leaders and manage orphaned students with clear admin controls.

📥

Reports & Data Imports

Support includes spreadsheet imports, CSV exports, Google Sheets sync and audit logging for safe data updates.

How it works

1

Students register

Sign up with full name, registration number, WhatsApp, gender, email, and programme details.

2

Form or join groups

Group creation rules, capacity limits and randomizer tools help keep the process balanced and fair.

3

Coordinators monitor

Faculty and selected coordinators can track coursework, publish tasks and intervene when needed.
