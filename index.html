Built for students & coordinators

👥

Group Formation

Create, browse, and join groups by course unit. Public or private visibility with join requests.

📋

Task Tracking

Per-coursework to-do lists: To-Do → In Progress → Submitted for both individual and group work.

📊

Live Oversight

Coordinators see real-time rosters, member counts, and can lock formation after deadlines.

🔄

Auto Sync

Every new group automatically updates a Google Sheet via Edge Functions for seamless admin tracking.

🛠️

Intervention Tools

View unassigned students, force-add/remove members, or auto-fill remaining seats randomly.

📥

Export & Reports

CSV/Excel downloads, filterable PDF reports, and bulk roster imports.

1

Students register

2

3

Coordinators monitor
